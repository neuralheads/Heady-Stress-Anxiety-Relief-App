## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Heady** ("we", "our", or "us"). Heady is a mental wellness application designed to help you manage stress, anxiety, and emotional well-being through guided breathing exercises, meditation, mood tracking, journaling, cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) tools, AI-powered support, and more.

Your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what choices you have. By using Heady, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

> **Important Notice Regarding Mental Health Data:** Heady handles sensitive mental health and wellness information. We apply the highest standards of care to this data and will never sell it to third parties or use it for advertising profiling.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Session activity data** — breathing sessions, meditation sessions, check-in frequency, streaks, and activity timestamps. Stored locally and used for your personal stats and insights.
- **App usage analytics** — screen views, feature usage events, and session durations, collected via **Firebase Analytics** (see Section 5).
- **Crash reports** — technical error details, device type, OS version, and app version, collected via **Firebase Crashlytics** (see Section 5).
- **Device identifiers** — anonymous advertising IDs used by **Google AdMob** for ad delivery (free tier only; see Section 5).
- **Push notification tokens** — device tokens used by **Firebase Cloud Messaging** for delivering reminders and alerts you have enabled.

### 2.3 Information Collected with Your Permission

- **Microphone** — Only accessed when you use the **Voice Journal** feature to record audio entries. Recordings are stored locally on your device and never uploaded unless you explicitly share them.
- **Biometric data** — If you enable biometric lock (Face ID / Fingerprint), your biometric data is processed entirely by your device's secure enclave via the operating system. We never receive or store raw biometric data.
- **Notifications** — We request permission to send local notifications for meditation reminders, medication reminders, daily check-in prompts, and goal reminders. You can manage or disable these at any time in your device settings.

### 2.4 Information from AI Features

When you use the **AI Chat** or **AI Insights** features:
- Conversation messages and journal text may be sent to a third-party AI processing service to generate responses or insights.
- This data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS).
- We use minimum-necessary data principles — we send only what is required to generate a response.
- AI conversation history is stored locally on your device.
- **We do not use your AI conversations to train AI models.**

When you use the **AI Therapy / Voice AI** feature powered by **LiveKit**:
- Audio is streamed in real time to process your voice session.
- Audio is not permanently stored by us after the session ends.
- Refer to LiveKit's privacy policy for their data handling practices.

## 4. Local-First Data Storage

Heady is built with a **local-first** architecture. The following data is stored **exclusively on your device** and is never transmitted to our servers:

- Journal entries (text and voice)
- Mood check-ins and history
- Breathing session logs
- Meditation session logs
- CBT thought records
- Gratitude entries
- Safety plan
- Goals and habits
- Medication details and reminders
- Trusted contacts
- Symptom logs
- Assessment responses
- Custom breathing patterns
- Profile information (name, photo)

This data is stored using **Hive** (a local encrypted database) and **SharedPreferences** on your device. **Deleting the app will permanently erase all locally stored data.** We recommend using your device backup features if you wish to preserve this data.

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services. Each has their own Privacy Policy which you should review.

### 5.1 Firebase (Google LLC)

We use the following Firebase products:

| Product | Data Collected | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Firebase Analytics** | Anonymous usage events, session data, device type, OS version | Understanding feature usage to improve the app |
| **Firebase Crashlytics** | Crash logs, device info, app version, stack traces | Detecting and fixing bugs |
| **Firebase Cloud Messaging** | Push notification tokens | Delivering reminders and alerts |

Firebase Analytics data is anonymized and aggregated. We do not share personally identifiable information with Firebase Analytics.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Firebase Data Processing Terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms)

### 5.2 Google AdMob (Free Tier Only)

If you are using the **free version** of Heady, we display ads powered by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect:
- Your device's advertising ID
- IP address (for geographic targeting)
- App usage signals

You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings:
- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

Premium subscribers do **not** see ads, and AdMob is not activated for premium accounts.

- [Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

### 5.3 In-App Purchases (Apple App Store / Google Play)

Subscription and purchase transactions are processed by **Apple** (iOS) or **Google** (Android) through their respective app store billing systems. We receive only transaction confirmation and subscription status — we never see your full payment card details.

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google Play Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 5.4 LiveKit

The **AI Voice** / real-time voice session feature is powered by **LiveKit**. Audio data is streamed in real time for processing. LiveKit handles this data in accordance with their privacy policy.

- [LiveKit Privacy Policy](https://livekit.io/privacy)

### 5.5 AI Language Model Provider

The **AI Chat** and **AI Insights** features may send text to an AI language model API. Text sent includes your chat messages and journal excerpts if you request AI insights. We never include your name, email, or identifying details in these API requests. Sessions are processed under data processing agreements that prohibit use of your data for model training.

## 6. Crisis Support & Safety

Heady includes **Crisis Support** and **Safety Plan** features for users who may be experiencing emotional distress. Please note:

- **Heady is not a medical service and is not a substitute for professional mental health care.**
- In a crisis, the app may display emergency hotline numbers and local emergency contacts — these are publicly available resources and no personal data is shared with them by Heady.
- The Safety Plan you create is stored **locally on your device only**.
- If you use the **Trusted Contacts** feature, contact details you enter are stored only on your device. Heady does not transmit these contacts to our servers.

## 9. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

- **Local data encryption** — all data stored on device is encrypted using Hive's encryption capabilities.
- **Transport encryption** — all data transmitted to third-party services uses TLS 1.2 or higher.
- **Biometric lock** — optional biometric authentication to prevent unauthorized access to the app.
- **Minimal data collection** — we collect only what is necessary for each feature.
- **No central database of personal wellness data** — your most sensitive data never leaves your device.

Despite these measures, no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use a device passcode and keep your operating system updated.

## 10. Your Rights and Choices

### 10.1 Access & Portability
You can view all your data directly within the app (Stats, Journal, Mood History, etc.). Since data is stored locally, you have direct access at all times.

### 10.2 Deletion
- **Delete all local data:** Go to **Settings → Delete All Data** in the app, or uninstall the app.
- **Delete analytics data:** Contact us (Section 13) to request erasure of any analytics identifiers associated with your device.

### 10.3 Opt-Out of Analytics
You can disable Firebase Analytics in **Settings → Privacy → Analytics** within the app.

### 10.4 Notification Control
Manage all notifications in **Settings → Notifications** within the app, or through your device system settings.

### 10.5 Ad Preferences
Control personalized advertising through your device advertising settings (see Section 5.2).

### 10.6 GDPR Rights (EEA / UK Users)
If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you have rights to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
- Restrict or object to processing
- Data portability
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

### 10.7 CCPA Rights (California Users)
California residents have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Know if personal information is sold (we do **not** sell it)
- Opt out of sale of personal information (not applicable)
- Request deletion of personal information
- Non-discrimination for exercising these rights
